HillFling Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DOORKNOB GAMING ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use HillFling ("the Game").

We aim to collect only information necessary to operate the Game, provide requested features, maintain security, and support advertising where permitted.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Game progress: Scores, coins, gems, unlocked cars, maps, achievements, preferences, and other saved game information.
  • Account information: If you choose to sign in, the authentication service may provide an account identifier and basic profile details, such as your name and email address.
  • Device and technical information: Operating system information, app version, device identifiers, technical diagnostics, and information needed to operate integrated services.
  • Advertising information: For eligible players, advertising partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions, consent choices, and related information necessary to deliver, measure, and protect ads.
  • Support communications: Information you voluntarily send to us when requesting assistance.

2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • Operate HillFling and its gameplay features.
  • Save and restore game progress.
  • Provide optional account and cloud-saving functionality.
  • Display advertising to eligible players.
  • Deliver eligible in-game advertising rewards.
  • Remember applicable privacy and consent choices.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Investigate technical problems and respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Saving

HillFling stores game progress and settings locally on your device. Certain features can operate without an account.

Eligible players may optionally use Google Sign-In and Firebase services to save and synchronize progress across supported devices.

Cloud saving uses an account identifier to associate saved progress with the correct player. If a cloud connection is unavailable, the Game may continue using local progress.

4. Advertising

HillFling uses Google AdMob to support the Game through advertisements where permitted.

Advertisements may include optional rewarded advertisements and advertisements between gameplay sessions.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, and consent signals, depending on applicable law, device settings, and advertising configuration.

We use applicable consent mechanisms before requesting advertisements where required. Eligible players may have access to advertising privacy choices through the Game's settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Children's Privacy

HillFling uses age-eligibility protections. Under the Game's current design, players identified as under 13 are not offered advertisements, account sign-in, or purchases.

Players whose age eligibility has not been established are also excluded from these restricted features.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact us using the email address below.

6. Third-Party Services

HillFling integrates services provided by Google, including Firebase, Google Sign-In, and Google AdMob.

These providers may process information to deliver their services, maintain security, and meet their applicable legal obligations. Their handling of information is also governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not sell player account information directly to data brokers.

7. Data Retention

Local game information generally remains on your device until it is deleted, cleared, or replaced.

Cloud progress and account-associated information are retained as needed to provide requested features and are subject to the Game's account-deletion procedures.

Limited information may be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or completion of deletion safeguards. Third-party providers may have their own retention periods.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Eligible signed-in players may request deletion of their HillFling account and associated cloud data through the Game's account-management features.

You may also contact us at doorknobgamingcompany@gmail.com with an account or data-deletion request.

We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request. Deletion of cloud data does not necessarily erase local game files remaining on a device.

9. Security

We use technical safeguards intended to protect saved information, including authenticated access controls for cloud-saving features.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your Choices

  • Play supported portions of the Game without signing in.
  • Choose whether to use optional cloud saving.
  • Manage available advertising consent preferences.
  • Request account or associated cloud-data deletion.
  • Contact us with privacy questions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when HillFling's features, service providers, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
