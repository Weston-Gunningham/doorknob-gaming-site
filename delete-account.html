Account and Data Deletion Requests

DOORKNOB GAMING respects your privacy and provides ways to request deletion of your Hill Fling account and associated personal data.

Option 1 — In-app deletion, where available

  1. Open Hill Fling on your device.
  2. Open the game's account or settings screen.
  3. Sign in to the account you want to delete, if necessary.
  4. If the app version provides a working account-deletion option, select it. If the option is not available or states that deletion cannot be completed, use the email request below instead.
  5. Follow the on-screen confirmation instructions.

Some versions may not yet support completion of deletion for managed, connected accounts while the server-side deletion process is finalized. A button or request does not by itself confirm that stored data was deleted. Use the email contact below to request help with the current account.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

You can request deletion without reinstalling Hill Fling or accessing the game.

  1. Email DOORKNOB GAMING using the button below.
  2. Use the subject line Hill Fling Account Deletion Request.
  3. Describe your request and provide sufficient information for us to locate and verify your Hill Fling account.
  4. We will respond with any verification steps necessary to process your request.

Request Account Deletion

Important: Never email us your password, authentication tokens, full payment-card information, or government-issued identification documents. We may request reasonable account verification, but we will not ask for your Google password.

What Information Is Deleted?

A verified deletion request concerns Hill Fling account data and any associated cloud-stored information to which the publisher's account-deletion process applies. Completion must be verified against the services storing that information. Some transaction, refund, security or fraud-prevention records may need to be retained separately.

This may include:

  • Cloud account identifiers associated with Hill Fling.
  • Cloud-saved scores and statistics.
  • Cloud-saved currency balances.
  • Cloud-saved car and map ownership.
  • Cloud-saved gameplay preferences and progress.

What Information May Remain?

The following information may remain after a deletion request:

  • Local game files stored on your device, unless separately cleared or uninstalled.
  • Limited deletion-protection records used temporarily to prevent stale cloud uploads from recreating deleted data.
  • Records that must be retained for applicable legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.
  • Information independently maintained by third-party providers under their own retention policies.

Historical cloud-deletion safeguards may use a short-lived blocking interval to prevent older devices from restoring deleted progress. The small protection record itself may remain stored beyond its active blocking interval until a supported cleanup procedure removes it. The final managed-account process and any retention exceptions must be verified separately.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

The current managed-account deletion process is still being verified for the final online-enabled version. An in-app option may therefore be unavailable or return a pending state instead of completing deletion. Email requests may require account verification and additional processing time.

For an email request, include a way for the publisher to contact you and ask for confirmation of processing and any applicable retention exceptions. Limited records may remain where reasonably necessary for security or legal obligations. No specific completion date is guaranteed by this page.

Deleting Local Game Data

Deleting your cloud account does not necessarily remove game progress saved locally on your phone.

To remove locally stored app data, you may need to clear the app's storage or uninstall Hill Fling using your device's operating-system settings.
