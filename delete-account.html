Account and Data Deletion Requests

DOORKNOB GAMING respects your privacy and provides ways to request deletion of your HillFling account and associated personal data.

Option 1 — Delete Your Account in HillFling

  1. Open HillFling on your device.
  2. Open the game's account or settings screen.
  3. Sign in to the account you want to delete, if necessary.
  4. Select the available account-deletion option.
  5. Follow the on-screen confirmation instructions.

Account deletion is intended to remove the account's associated cloud-save information and prevent the deleted account from continuing to use that saved data.

Option 2 — Request Deletion by Email

You can request deletion without reinstalling HillFling or accessing the game.

  1. Email DOORKNOB GAMING using the button below.
  2. Use the subject line HillFling Account Deletion Request.
  3. Describe your request and provide sufficient information for us to locate and verify your HillFling account.
  4. We will respond with any verification steps necessary to process your request.

Request Account Deletion

You may also contact us directly at: doorknobgamingcompany@gmail.com

Important: Never email us your password, authentication tokens, full payment-card information, or government-issued identification documents. We may request reasonable account verification, but we will not ask for your Google password.

What Information Is Deleted?

When an eligible account-deletion request is completed, the associated HillFling cloud account information and cloud-saved game progress are scheduled for deletion under the game's established deletion procedure.

This may include:

  • Cloud account identifiers associated with HillFling.
  • Cloud-saved scores and statistics.
  • Cloud-saved currency balances.
  • Cloud-saved car and map ownership.
  • Cloud-saved gameplay preferences and progress.

What Information May Remain?

The following information may remain after a deletion request:

  • Local game files stored on your device, unless separately cleared or uninstalled.
  • Limited deletion-protection records used temporarily to prevent stale cloud uploads from recreating deleted data.
  • Records that must be retained for applicable legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.
  • Information independently maintained by third-party providers under their own retention policies.

The game's current cloud-deletion safeguards may maintain a limited deletion-protection record for approximately 24 hours. Under the current free-tier configuration, that minimal record may remain stored after its blocking period expires until manually removed or another supported cleanup method is enabled.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

In-app deletion is processed through the game's existing account-deletion mechanism. Email requests require identity verification and may require additional processing time.

We will communicate the status of an email request and any applicable retention exceptions. Limited records may remain where reasonably necessary for security or legal obligations.

Deleting Local Game Data

Deleting your cloud account does not necessarily remove game progress saved locally on your phone.

To remove locally stored app data, you may need to clear the app's storage or uninstall HillFling using your device's operating-system settings.
