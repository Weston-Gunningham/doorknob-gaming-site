Playing the game

Hold the left side of the screen to rotate backward during a jump, and hold the right side to dive. Line up your wheels with the slope to land safely. Complete the tutorial on your first run. Use the Garage to choose vehicles and Settings to adjust available options.

Core gameplay can be played offline. Connected accounts, invitations, purchases and optional rewarded ads depend on the app version, eligibility, network access and service availability.

Progress or purchase problems

If your progress looks wrong, avoid clearing app storage or uninstalling the game until you have checked your saved progress and account status. If a store purchase or ad reward appears pending, use the in-app recovery controls when available and contact us with the app version and steps to reproduce the issue. Refunds and billing are managed through your Apple App Store or Google Play purchase account.

Contact DOORKNOB GAMING

Email: doorknobgamingcompany@gmail.com

For a technical issue, include your device model, operating system, Hill Fling version, affected screen and what happened. Screenshots may help if you hide account and purchase details.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card numbers, private security keys or government ID documents by email.

Privacy or deletion requests

See the Privacy Policy for information about game data, or Account Deletion for an outside-app deletion request. Account data and local device progress may need separate steps to remove.
